This map shows the sky from any date and time from 1500 to 2500 AD and any location on Earth, with bright stars to magnitude ~6.5 on mobile, more stars to magnitude 9 on desktop, the Messier and Caldwell deep sky objects, and planet positions to arcminute accuracy. Asteroids and comet data are from the Minor Planet Center.
